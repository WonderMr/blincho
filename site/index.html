A few things to know

Before your first pause.

Is Blincho really free?

Yes. Every feature is free to use, with no paid tier. Optional donations help support the project and never unlock extra features.

What happens when I leave my computer?

Blincho pauses break timing when you are away, your screen is locked or your computer is asleep, then continues when you return.

Which computers can run Blincho?

Builds are available for macOS, Windows and Linux. Windows and Linux behavior on real hardware has not yet been verified. Pick the architecture and package that match your computer.

Can I make it fit my routine?

Yes. You can adjust break preferences, choose a theme and switch between 30 languages in the app.
